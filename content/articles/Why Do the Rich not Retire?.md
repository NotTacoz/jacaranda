Why Do the Rich not Retire?

Throughout my life, I have heard the concept of retirement dancing around in many discussions. Many people, young and old, dreams and idealises a time where they could no longer work - where they could have the money, and the time, and the love and compassion to give to people.

But retirement can only be reached if you have the money, so I thought. Your retirement age should be as low as possible, so I thought. So, the richest people would have already retired? 

I know that isn't true. There are many rich people who CAN, but WON'T retire. And by rich, I mean the super wealthy - people who can afford anything and everything, not just enough to retire, but they can retire a thousand times over if they so desire. People like Warren Buffet, Elon Musk, Steve Jobs - that kind of rich and successful.
