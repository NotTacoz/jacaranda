proteins
- diverse group of large and complex polymer molecules, made up of long chains of amino acids.
- amino acids contain both amine (nh2) and carboxyl (cooh) functional group
- alpha carbon
	- second carbon on the amino acid chain after carboxylic acid
	- O=OH-C-C <- that carbon is alpha
	- attached to amine fr
- alphatic
	- hydrocarbon chains
- amino acids are a acid and a base (what!?!??)
- zwitterion
## peptide formation
using amino acids as monomers to make polymers
- amide/peptide link/group
- peptide bond
- 2 amino acid: dipepetide
- 3 amino acid: tripeptide
- many amino acids: polypeptide
- protein is only when polypeptide in functional.
- chain of amino acid monomers joined together to form a polypeptide
- condensation reaction.
- does a lot of stuff
	- structural : main component of body tissues, such as muscle, skin, ligaments and hair
	- catalytic: all enzymes are proteins, catalysing many biochemical reaection.
	- immunological: all antibodies are proteins
- diverse range of antibodies protects us good.!!!!!!
-  primary structure: order of amino acid residues along the chain
	- used covalent bonding
- secondary structure : regular structures such a-helicies and b-sheets
- tertiary structures: 3D arrangements of the substructures to give overall shapes
- quaternary structure: subunits combine to give active protein.
- the only way to open the primary structure is hydrolyse it open to break it down
	- very stable
	- held together by covalent bonds
## secondary structure
- alpha helicies and beta sheets have good hydrogen bonding
- it is a polyamide with hydrogen bonding!
- very strong dipole because it zigzags like wmwmw and alternates delta+ and delta-
- the bonds are not covalent
- r groups poke out of the way out of a-helicies (dont add them to drawing)
- r groups on the whole point outwards
- r groups above and below plane of sheet
