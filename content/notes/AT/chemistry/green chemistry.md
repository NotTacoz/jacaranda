## the greenhouse effect
- ![[notes/images/Pasted image 20240312134635.png]]
	- diagram
- visible light and solar radiation and infrared comes from sun.
	- bulk of energy is visible light.
- visible light passes through the atmosphere and absorbed by the surface of the earth. increases its temperature.
- hot objects emits infrared radiation
	- most infrared is not absorbed in the atmosphere and leaves the earth's surface.
- molecules of GHGs absorb infrared radiation
- molecules increase vibrational energy
- passes energy as kinetic energy.
- greenhouse gases
	- h2o water vapor
	- co2 carbon dioxide
	- ch4 methane
	- n2o dinitrogen monoxide
	- o3 ozone
	- cfcs chlorofluorocarbons
	- hfcs hydrofluorocarbons
- contribution
	- concentration of gas in atmosphere
		- not ozone
	- intensity of interaction with infrared
	- lifetime of gas in atmosphere
