## % yield calcs
- yield compared to theoretical max
- always check if you identified the limiting reagent!!!!!
	- and if you had actually used the limiting reagent number of moles
- give it as a percentage!!!!!!!!
- multiply the percentage of reaction series.

### Formula.
$$\text{yield}=\frac{\text{actual yield}}{\text{theoreatical yield}} \times 100 $$
note: in multiple processes, you multiply the yield together..!
## CAN WE EVER REACH 100% (yield.)
- NO.
- reasons being:
	- equilibrium will not go to completion.
	- excess of cheaper reagent means not all reagent will be used
	- reagents might do unwanted side reaction -> creating useless by-product
	- reagents are impure, results in less product than expected.
	- impossible to physically separate and purify all of the product form.

## Atom Economy
- is ethene hydration: 100% of the reactants end up in the products. (addition)
- atom economy is 100% if you see no waste products. if you do see waste products, WITHOUT calcs you can FOR SURE conclude that there is no 100% atom economy.

### How to Calculate?
$$\text{atom economy}=\frac{\text{mass of desired products} \times 100}{ \text{total mass of reactants}}$$
