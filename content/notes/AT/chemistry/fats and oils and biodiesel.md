## fats
- fats and oils are the same thing in the chemistry sense.
- part of the groups of esters called triglycerides.
- saturated fats - there are no c=c double bonds; straight fatty acids chains pack close together: so it has stronger ( relatively) dispersion force. as a result, often, saturated fats are solids at room temperature.
	- DONT TALK ABOUT SURFACE AREA - molecules DO NOT have a surface as they do not have an edge
- unsaturated fats: at least one c=c double bond. mostly (entirely) cis double bonds (99.9%~ naturally occurring are cis)
### how is margarine made?
- chemical name for any spread that started out as oil and gets turned into a spread.
- bubble hydron gas through a huge bubble (of what?)
- use solid metal, catalyst like metal nikcel (like small pieces)
- nickel reacts with the carbon carbon double bond
- partially hydrogenate some of the carbon carbon bonds to make it slightly less .
- margarines are only partially hyodgenated, because if it reduces more double bond, it wil be hard when coming out of the fridge.
- sometimes double form reforms if nothing replaces the double bond. but might not reform in the same isomers
- for example, cis might not make trans
- becuase we do not have a good enzyme to break isomer energy. - so we end up with a lot of trans fats. and trans fats build up in your arteries and stuff.
- diet high in trans fat increases risk of coronary heart disease
