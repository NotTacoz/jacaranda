## what is soap
- if oil is added to water, the two liquids DO NOT mix. which makes it hard to remove.
- emulsion: droplets of one liquid separated in another
- emulsifier: encourage emulsion
- form micelles : tiny droplets of oil mixed in water
- agitate the soap and oil
- does not work with the agitation
## the dangers of hard water
- hard water is the counter to soap
- acidic rainwater dissolves carbon dioxide to form water in the ground (/?/w/2/2/?????/)
- limestoney area contains lots of calcium carbonate, and reacts with the acidic rainwater, which trickles through the rocks and forms calcium hydrogen carbonate.
- hard water is defined as water that contains the following:
	- magnesium hydrogen carbonate
	- calcium hydrogen carbonate
	- magnesium sulfate
	- calcium sulfate
- hard water can be temporary or permanent (YOU DO NO NEED BUT USEFUL GENERAL KNOWLEDGE)
	- temporary decomposes on heating (when it contains magnesium hydrogen carbonate and calcium hydrogen carbonate)
	- permanent (mg sulfate, ca sulfate) do not decompose :(
- hard water and soaps are not friends
- soap is scum is formed by precipitation with hard water.

hence, the need for detergent!
## the need for detergent
- sulphonate group instead of a carboxylate group form detergents, which does not precipitate with magnesium hydrogen carbonate or other stuff in hard water. exciting
- does not react with hard water because there is no precipitation reaction with hard water. also cheaper.
- not animal or plant product so it is not biodegradable.
- biological detergents are soapless detergents that contain enzymes
	- enzymes break down chemicals, e.g. in stains on clothes
