## aldehydes and ketones
- carbonyl group (>C=O) is the functional group founded in compounds such as aldehydes, ketones and carboxylic acid.
	- aldehydes: carbonyl group is a the end of the carbon chan and so has at least one hydrogen attached to it.
	- ketones: carbonyl group is in the middle of a carbon chain and has two alkyl group attached to it.
	- aldehydes and ketones can be isomers. because they have a different functional group, they are called functional groups.
	- CHO represents aldehyde as OH represents hydroxide
- count from the aldehyde - as they are the most important
- carbonyl is polar due to greater electronegativity of oxygen
	- strongest imf in aldehyde and ketone is dipole dipole forces.
	- water can do hydrogen bonding with carbonyl group!!
	- cant form hydrogen bond but can receive hydrogen bonding
