## Torque
>[!note] Dr Waters thinks its hardest topic in Year 12. How fun.
- There has to be a question on Torque in all future tests.
	- It will be there!
- Object must be in translational equilibrium i.e. $\Sigma F=\phi$
- rotational i.e. $\Sigma \tau=\phi$
- Torque = moment = turning force
- STAWA refers to torque as moment; however syllabus refers to torque as torque
- $\tau = F r \sin \theta$; $\tau_{max}=Fr_{\perp}$
- $\tau$ = torque (N m)
- $F$ = Force (N)
- r = length of level arm *distance of point of application of Force perpendicular to axis of rotation*
- You must need a direction! Unless you have been asked only the magnitude.
![[notes/images/Screenshot 2024-02-27 at 11.25.52 am.png|300]]
- For any given length of meter arm, torque will be increased by increasing the $F_{\perp}$. For a given force, torque will be increased by increasing length of level arm $\tau_{\perp}$.
- For give combination of F o r, torque will be max; missed by having them applied perpendicular to each other.
- You can use cw for clockwise and acw for anticlockwise.
