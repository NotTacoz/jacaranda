- 2 types of charge +ve, -ve, neutral
- for objects made up of charged particles, 
	- +ve if more +ve charges than -ve charges
	- -ve if more -ve charges than +ve charges
	- neutral if same number of +ve nd -ve charges
- neutral objects made up of many charges will always experience attractive forces in an electric field, regardless of the nature of the charge of the particle producing the field due to redistribution of charges that make up the neutral object.
