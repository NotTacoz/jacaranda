## bohr model of the atom
- electrons travel in circular shells (energy levels)
- they do so because of centripetal force acting them by the  positively charge electrons
- they dont fall into the nucleus and they also lose energy
- each atom has a number of shell with fixed distances from the nucleus.
- the radii of the shells of an atom vary with the element.
- the neutron state for an atom is to be in its ground state if the electrons are as close to the nucleus as possible
- electrons can transition between energy levels, but nowhere else.
