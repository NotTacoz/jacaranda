## dake
- several ways to say only
	- but only 2 in jap
- na adj ~ dake
- i adj/noun ~ dake
- verbs (plain) ~ dake
- replaces ga and wo
- other particles come after dake
- u also hear not only
	- dake de ha naku~mo
	- dakejanaku~mo
- dekirudake
