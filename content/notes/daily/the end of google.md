- i predict that google in the future will definitely be replaced - whether it be generative AI or some other better platform
- the reason for this prediction is as follows:
	 1. google is getting lazier and lazier as they keep holding this search monopoly which they had for decades
		 1. google is considered the retirement home for senior engineers, which is pretty hilarious.
	1. while they are being lazier, better more efficient platforms has emerged with improved searching, as general searching has become more niche
		1. you use tiktok to search for tutorials, you use wikipedia for articles, you use youtube for videos. the only times i have personally used google in the past year is 1. basic facts and statistics 2. images, but these are two things which I have found to increasingly get worse over time.
	2. generative ai
		1. will generative ai fully replace search? No - but the nuance of search perfectly tailored to your requirements is long gone. the ai we use now is the worst version of AI we will see in the future, and as it gets better it means that it will be more and more nuanced, fit better and better with your requirements and what you want - and generally replacing the more inefficient google which requires multiple searches to come up with an answer that you don't really care about.
- those are the 3 fundamental reasons i predict google with phase out in the future - this is only for google search and other sectors of google is remaining profitable, youtube is growing and so is googles monopoly on ads. however, the search monopoly google has is definitely being challenged, and the monopoly they hold in it will definitely be challenged.
